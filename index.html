2026 Kalimantan Wildfires

Hermanto. C. H, (2026)

A snapshot of the worst wildfire season in the ‘lungs of the world’ since 2015. Occurring in Indonesia, the haze has reached Singapore, Malaysia, and other ASEAN countries.

Your grandfather used to gather rattan from the forest and sell them to make furniture, that’s how he kept our family alive. It used to have tall large trees and dense bushes, if you weren’t careful you’d run into these striped snakes called “ular birang”. Your uncle played with them before he realized they were venomous.

We have very specific wet soil here, called “gambut” or peat. It held so much carbon from thousands of years of dead plants that we used to dry them and to burn when we couldn’t find coal. We’ve come far since then, we own our store now.

But then the businessman came and burnt down the forests, all they cared about were the palm trees and the oil.

This July was so dry, I couldn’t leave the home without AC and slowly the sky turned orange. No one ever found who lit the first flame, but we know its one of these palm plantations, they don’t grow well in wet lands, every summer they burn the land.
